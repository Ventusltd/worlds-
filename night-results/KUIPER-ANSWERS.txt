THE KUIPER ANSWER TABLE
======================================================================

Every answer worked out in advance, so that reading this needs no
program, no account and nobody's help. If you are holding this and
everyone who made it is gone, it still answers.

WHAT THIS IS ABOUT. Solar panels wired end to end in a row add up
their voltage, and COLD WEATHER RAISES IT. Every panel carries a
printed figure for the most voltage its system may carry. So there
is a limit to how many you may put in one row, and that limit is
set by the coldest morning of the year, not the hottest afternoon.
Separately, sunlight bouncing off the ground onto the backs of the
panels adds CURRENT, and current is judged when the panels are hot.
Voltage is a cold problem. Current is a hot one. Both are below.

HOW MUCH WAS TRIED. 197,638,099,200 combinations of panel type, panels in a row,
rows joined together, morning temperature, panel temperature and
rear light. Not a sample of them. That many, one at a time.

HOW TO DISBELIEVE THIS PROPERLY.

Every figure was worked out twice, by two methods written to be
different from one another. Where they disagreed, nothing at all
was published for that case. They disagreed on 0 cases here.

A count is meaningless without knowing what it is a count OF, so:
the figures above come from 197,638,099,200 combinations out of a possible
197,638,099,200. That is 1.0 of the whole.

WHAT IS DELIBERATELY MISSING, and hold it against us.

This work measures against four limits. Two are printed on the
manufacturers' documents and anyone may check them. TWO COULD NOT
BE TRACED TO ANY DOCUMENT AT ALL, and one of those two turned out
to be the same test as a third, written twice with the number
halved. So there are three limits, not four, and one of the three
is invented. No answer above rests on either untraceable one. That
is why some questions are missing rather than answered badly.

THIS IS NOT ENGINEERING. It says whether a design sits inside a
rating printed by the people who made the equipment. It is not an
offer of connection, not an assessment of whether a thing can be
built, and not a design anyone may consent. Engineering carries
the name of a person who answers for it. Nothing here does.

No site, client, project, place, manufacturer or model is named in
this file, and none is needed in order to check it.
