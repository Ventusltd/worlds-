THE KUIPER ANSWER TABLE
======================================================================

Every answer worked out in advance, so that reading this needs no
program, no account and nobody's help. If you are holding this and
everyone who made it is gone, it still answers.

WHAT IT ANSWERS. A row of solar panels wired end to end adds up its
voltage, and cold weather RAISES that voltage. Every panel has a
figure printed on it for the most voltage its system may carry. So
there is a limit to how many you may put in one row, and the limit
is set by the coldest morning, not the hottest afternoon. That is
the question below, asked four ways.

HOW THESE WERE WORKED OUT, so you can disbelieve them properly.

Every count above was found twice, by two methods written to be
different from each other. Where the two disagreed, nothing was
published at all - not a warning, not a footnote. A number that
cannot be got twice is not reported here.

12,000,000,600 combinations were examined at 20000001 temperature points across
the range -30.0 to 10.0 degrees.

WHAT IS DELIBERATELY MISSING, and you should hold this against us.

This work measures against four limits. Two of them are printed on
the manufacturers' own documents and can be checked by anyone who
obtains those documents. TWO OF THEM COULD NOT BE TRACED TO ANY
DOCUMENT AT ALL - and one of those two turned out to be the same
test as a third, written twice. No answer above rests on either of
the untraceable ones. That is why some questions are not answered
here rather than answered badly.

The current a row carries is a separate question from how many
panels fit in it, and it is not answered here.

THIS IS NOT ENGINEERING. It says whether a row of panels sits
inside a rating printed by the people who made them. It is not a
connection offer, not an assessment of whether a thing can be
built, and not a design anyone may consent. Engineering carries
the name of a person who is answerable for it. Nothing here does.

No site, client, project, place, manufacturer or model is named in
this file, and none is needed to check it.
