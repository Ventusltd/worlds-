THE KUIPER CIRCUIT TABLE
======================================================================

Every circuit that can be built from identical parts, and what each
one adds up to. Worked out in advance, so that reading this needs no
program, no account and nobody's help. If you are holding this and
everyone who made it is gone, it still answers.

THE WHOLE OF IT IN TWO SENTENCES. Join parts END TO END and their
voltages add while the current stays as one part's. Join them SIDE
BY SIDE and their currents add while the voltage stays as one
part's. Every power system built from repeated parts is those two
moves, nested - so the list of possible circuits is finite, and what
each one does is fixed by arithmetic rather than by judgement.

HOW MANY WERE TRIED. 5,574,220,800 circuits, across 7,680 shapes per part type
and every combination of morning temperature and part temperature.
Not a sample of them. That many, one at a time.

HOW TO DISBELIEVE THIS PROPERLY

Every figure was worked out twice, by two methods written to be
different from one another. Where they disagreed, nothing at all
was published for that circuit. They disagreed on 0.

A count means nothing without knowing what it counts OUT OF: these
come from 5,574,220,800 circuits of a possible 5,574,220,800.

WHAT 'ALL POSSIBLE CIRCUITS' HONESTLY MEANS, because the phrase can
be stretched. This covers every SERIES-PARALLEL arrangement: any
circuit built by joining groups end to end or side by side. That is
the shape of essentially every power system made from repeated
parts. It does NOT cover bridge circuits, meshed networks or rings,
where a path exists that is neither. Those need a different method
and nothing here pretends to them.

WHAT IS DELIBERATELY MISSING, and hold it against us. This work
measures against four limits. Two are printed on the manufacturers'
documents and anyone may check them. TWO COULD NOT BE TRACED TO ANY
DOCUMENT AT ALL, and one of those two turned out to be the same
test as a third, written twice with the number halved. So there are
three limits, not four, and one of the three is invented. No answer
above rests on either untraceable one.

RINGS, because most of a power system is not a straight line

A ring feeds from a source both ways round and meets itself. The
current divides between the two paths, so no single length carries
all of it - which is the whole reason rings are built. But a ring
is only a ring while it is CLOSED. Break it anywhere and the whole
load goes the long way round, and every length must be able to
carry that. A ring sized only for the closed case is two dead ends
pretending to be a ring.

We do not warrant that the arithmetic is correct. We do not warrant
that the physics is correct. We do not warrant that the current
flows the way this assumes, that the parts behave as their
documents say, or that any figure here applies to anything you
intend to build. We warrant nothing at all.

Checking is the reader's job, and it is why everything here is
written down in the open instead of summarised: every figure, the
method behind it, and what could not be traced. A summary asks for
trust. This asks to be checked, and gives you what you need to do
it. If you use a number from this file without checking it, that
choice and its consequences are entirely yours.

THIS IS NOT ENGINEERING. It says what a circuit adds up to and
whether that sits inside a rating printed by the people who made
the parts. Engineering carries the name of a person who answers for
it. Nothing here does.

No site, client, project, place, manufacturer or model is named in
this file, and none is needed in order to check it.
